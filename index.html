⚠️ Medical Red Flags

Consult a doctor immediately if you have:

  • Sudden, rapidly progressive, or fluctuating hearing loss
  • Active drainage, bleeding, pressure, or pain in the ear
  • Visible foreign objects or significant wax buildup
  • Tinnitus (ringing), dizziness, imbalance, or vertigo
  • Facial weakness or facial nerve paralysis
  • One-sided hearing loss or major difference between ears
  • History of loud noise trauma or ear injury
  • Persistent fullness or blocked sensation in the ear
  • Difficulty understanding speech even when sounds seem loud enough
  • Any sudden change or specific concern regarding your hearing
